# Ordered Optimal Classification

Ordinal responses (a survey's agree-disagree items, a court's graded
outcomes) classified by item directions and cutpoints in the spirit of
Hare, Liu and Lupton (2018): respondents and item directions start
normal (seed 42, as [`set.seed()`](https://rdrr.io/r/base/Random.html)
takes it), the cutpoints of each item at equal steps on \\\[-1, 1\]\\;
each sweep moves every cutpoint to the median of the midpoints between
neighbouring respondents (in projection order) on either side of it,
until the share classified correctly stops changing.

## Usage

``` r
ordocl(Y, ndim = 2, maxit = 500)
```

## Arguments

- Y:

  A respondent-by-item matrix of ordinal responses, `NA` for missing.

- ndim:

  The number of dimensions.

- maxit:

  The most sweeps.

## Value

A list of class `ordocl`: `ideal`, `cuts` (one vector per item),
`normals`, `correct` (the share classified correctly) and `iter`.

## References

Hare, C., Liu, T.-P. and Lupton, R. N. (2018). What Ordered Optimal
Classification reveals about ideological structure, cleavages, and
polarization in the American mass public. *Public Choice*, 176(1),
57-78.
