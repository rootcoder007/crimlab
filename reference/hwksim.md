# Hawkes process: simulation

Simulates a Hawkes process on `[0, hor]` by its branching construction
(Hawkes and Oakes 1974): background events from the baseline (by
thinning for the `"cycle"` baseline), and from each event a Poisson
number, mean `eta`, of offspring at lags drawn from the kernel, and so
on over the generations. With `win` the process is in space and time:
background events fall uniformly in the rectangle, the baseline is then
per unit area, and each offspring lands at a normal offset (standard
deviation `sigma`) from its parent; offspring outside the window or past
`hor` are not recorded and have no offspring of their own.

## Usage

``` r
hwksim(
  par,
  hor,
  kernel = "exp",
  base = "const",
  per = 365.25,
  win = NULL,
  sigma = NULL,
  seed = NULL,
  nmax = 1e+05
)
```

## Arguments

- par:

  A fit from
  [`hwkfit()`](https://rootcoder007.github.io/crimlab/reference/hwkfit.md)
  or
  [`sthwk()`](https://rootcoder007.github.io/crimlab/reference/sthwk.md),
  or parameters as `par` of
  [`hwkfit()`](https://rootcoder007.github.io/crimlab/reference/hwkfit.md)
  (a space-time fit gives `win` and `sigma` too).

- hor:

  The end of the window: one positive number.

- kernel, base, per:

  As for
  [`hwkfit()`](https://rootcoder007.github.io/crimlab/reference/hwkfit.md);
  one kernel and one baseline (not read with a fit).

- win:

  `NULL`, or the rectangle `c(xmin, xmax, ymin, ymax)` of a space-time
  process.

- sigma:

  The spread of offspring in space with `win`: one positive number.

- seed:

  `NULL` to draw the seed from R's generator, or one whole number from
  0.

- nmax:

  The most events simulated: one whole number from 1 (more is an error).

## Value

A data frame in time order: `t`, with `x` and `y` in space, and `gen`,
the generation (0 for a background event).

## References

Hawkes, A. G. and Oakes, D. (1974). A cluster process representation of
a self-exciting process. *Journal of Applied Probability*, 11(3),
493-503. [doi:10.2307/3212693](https://doi.org/10.2307/3212693)
