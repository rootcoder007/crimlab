# Cutting lines of votes in two dimensions

The ends of each vote's cutting line \\n_1 x + n_2 y = c\\ across the
horizontal range `xlim` (or from -10 to 10 vertically when the line is
vertical), its midpoint and the angle of its normal: the lines of a
Coombs mesh (Poole 2005). The plot itself is not drawn here.

## Usage

``` r
cutlns(normals, cuts, xlim = c(-1, 1))
```

## Arguments

- normals:

  The normal vectors of the votes (votes by 2).

- cuts:

  Each vote's cutpoint \\c\\.

- xlim:

  The horizontal range.

## Value

A list of class `cutlns`: `ends` (votes by 4: x1, y1, x2, y2), `angles`
(degrees), `mids` (votes by 2) and `nlines`.

## References

Poole, K. T. (2005). *Spatial Models of Parliamentary Voting*. Cambridge
University Press.
