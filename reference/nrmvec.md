# Normal vector of an outside measure

Projects an outside measure (a judge's party, a legislator's vote share)
into an ideal-point space: the least-squares regression of the measure
on the coordinates, its slopes scaled to unit length give the direction
along which the measure rises, with its angle and the \\R^2\\ (Armstrong
et al. 2021, section 2.6).

## Usage

``` r
nrmvec(x, y)
```

## Arguments

- x:

  Ideal points (units by dimensions).

- y:

  The outside measure, `NA` for missing.

## Value

A list of class `nrmvec`: `normal`, `degrees`, `radians`, `r2` and
`coef` (intercept first).

## References

Armstrong, D. A., Bakker, R., Carroll, R., Hare, C., Poole, K. T. and
Rosenthal, H. (2021). *Analyzing Spatial Models of Choice and Judgment*,
2nd ed. Chapman & Hall/CRC.
